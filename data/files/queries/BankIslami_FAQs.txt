Q-1: What is the Vision of Bank Islami?
Ans: The vision of BankIslami is promoting global economic prosperity based on Islamic financial system.

Q-2: What is the mission of Bank Islami?
Ans: The Mission of BankIslami is Saving Humanity from Riba by offering Shariah compliant, customer-centric, innovative financial solutions and creating value for our stakeholders while       upholding social responsibility and transparency.

Q-3: Minimun Balance to open Account in BankIslami?
Ans: A minimum balance of Rs. 100/- only to open the account.

Q-4: Cost of getting account statement?
Ans: You can get Free Account statement.

Q-5: How do I sign up for internet/mobile banking?
Ans: You can sign up yourself once your bank account is active and a debit card has been issued.

Q-6: Will Zakat be deducted from my account?
Ans: Yes, Zakat is deductible on all Savings accounts unless a Zakat Declaration Form (CZ50) is uploaded to the portal before the 1st of Shabaan.

Q-7: How can I update my personal information (e.g., mobile number, email address)?
Ans: You need to visit your nearest BankIslami branch to update personal information in their records.

Q-8: What are the limits for Digital Accounts?
Ans: For an Islami Asaan Digital Account, the monthly debit limit is PKR 1,000,000/- and the total credit balance limit is PKR 1,000,000/-.

Q-9: How does "One Touch Banking" work?
Ans: Customers with registered biometric records can conduct over-the-counter and ATM transactions using only their thumbprint, without needing a physical card or PIN for branch transactions. 

Q-10: How do I activate my new debit card?
Ans: Debit cards are deactivated by default. You must call BankIslami Phone Banking at (021) 111-475-264 from your registered mobile number to activate it after verification.

Q-11: What if my card is lost or stolen?
Ans: Immediately call the BankIslami Phone Banking number (021) 111-475-264 to report it lost/stolen and request a replacement. You can also block the card via the mobile app or internet banking.

Q-12: How do I change my Card PIN?
You can change your card PIN at any BankIslami ATM by inserting your card and using the biometric verification device.

Q-13: Do I need to visit my selected branch for document verification?
Ans: You are not required to visit any BankIslami branch for physical verification of your documents.

Q-14: How can I download the BankIslami on my smart-phone?
Ans: BankIslami can be downloaded from Apple Store or Google Play Store on your smartphones by simply typing BankIslami in the search bar.

Q-15: What if I forget my Password?
Ans: You can reset your password through BankIslami by using “Forgot password” option and then you need to visit any BankIslami Branch or contact 111-ISLAMI (111-475-264) for activation of services.

Q-16: What if I enter the wrong password?
Ans: If you enter the wrong password consecutively (3) times, your services will be de-activated. For Re-activation of services you need to visit any BankIslami Branch or contact 111-ISLAMI (111-475-264) from your registered contact number.

Q-17: Is BankIslami service safe to use?
Ans: Yes! BankIslami is a safe and secure way of making payments. No information is stored on your mobile phone which means that your CNIC & Password is never exposed even if your mobile handset falls in the wrong hands. All transactions are secured with Advanced Encryption Methodologies. In addition, to conduct any non-beneficiary transaction on BankIslami; an OTP is sent to your registered Mobile Number/email or on both.

Q-18: Is there any transaction limit?
Ans: Yes! Funds transfer limit per transaction is Rs. 100,000/- & Rs.500,000/- per day. These limits may change as per Bank discretion. BankIslami will update any change in its limit via social media channels
